Surf

A current Chromium browser for devices that can no longer run one.

Chromium runs on your computer. Surf streams each page to an app on your phone, tablet, PC or handheld, and the app sends your taps and typing back.

Download Surf See the clients ›

Pages load on your computer

Sites see a recent desktop version of Chromium, so pages that no longer open on an old device work normally.

Each client is a native app

Tabs, the address bar, bookmarks, history and downloads are drawn by the app, using the controls of the device it runs on.

Only your devices connect

Each device is paired with your computer and keeps its own key. There is no account and no hosted service.

How Surf works

The Surf host runs on Windows, macOS or Linux and keeps a Chromium profile with your cookies, sign-ins and downloads. A client connects to it over TLS, shows the page as video with sound, and sends touch, keyboard and pointer input back. Every client talks to the host the same way, so they all share the same tabs, bookmarks and history.

Taps, typing and uploaded files

If a site is hard to sign in to from a device, open the same browser on the computer with Browser setup, sign in there, and continue on the device.

Clients

Each client pairs with the same host. The client core is shared, so new platforms can be added without changing the host.

Pairing a device

Open an invitation on the computer, then scan its QR code with the app or type in the address and six-digit code.

Both screens then show the same six words. If they match, nothing is sitting between the device and your computer. You can revoke a device from the computer at any time.

Requirements

Host computer
64-bit Windows, macOS 12 or later, or Linux on x86_64 or ARM.
Browser on the host
Surf uses Chrome, Edge or Chromium if one is installed, and downloads Chromium otherwise.
Clients
iOS 6 through 14 with a rootful jailbreak, Linux with X11 or Wayland, and the PS Vita through a community port.
Network
Works on your home network, or from any address the device can reach. The connection uses TLS pinned to the host's certificate.
Protected video
DRM video plays only when the installed browser provides Widevine. Surf doesn't bundle it.
