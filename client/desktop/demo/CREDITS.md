# Surf repository demo — sources and review notes

This is a review draft, not a published video. It shows the real Linux client
using a 768×1024 portrait viewport, not filmed iOS hardware. Surf's iOS app has
its own native interface. The product serves legacy iPhone, iPad and iPod touch
devices; the recording preset is not a claim about performance on every device.

Input passes through the actual client and backend. Read-only browser inspection
locates controls and checks playback. Footage runs at normal speed, with cuts,
uniform scaling, captions, and brief audio fades. The pointer is excluded by
the screen recorder. There is no fake interface, interpolated motion, voiceover,
or substituted soundtrack. Audio comes from Surf client playback; the Netflix
excerpt has an 8 dB level increase, without a timing shift.

MP4/poster and appropriate credits. Nothing is uploaded automatically.
